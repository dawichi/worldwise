# Scoring is correctness-based with per-mode partial credit; time is recorded but not scored

Scores are based on correctness, with partial credit only where a mode has a natural notion of "how close": Compass gives reduced credit for a near-miss direction (an adjacent compass point), Arrange gives proportional credit for a partially-correct sequence, and Border Run stays binary (a route is valid or it isn't — a connectivity puzzle has no natural partial-credit notion). Elapsed time is recorded per round for reference and future display, but does not affect the score. This matches the product's stated MVP priority of correctness over speed and keeps the loop accessible, since time pressure and thoughtful play are never penalized. The alternative of a uniform distance-based formula across all modes was rejected because "distance" means something different in each mode and would feel arbitrary.
