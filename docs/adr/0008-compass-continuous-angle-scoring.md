# Compass answers are a continuous angle, scored by angular error, not a discrete 8-direction choice

ADR-0004 described Compass as giving "reduced credit for a near-miss direction (an adjacent compass point)," assuming the player chooses from eight discrete direction buttons. We decided instead that a player indicates their answer by positioning an indicator anywhere on a continuous circular dial, and credit is a continuous function of the angular error between that answer and the true great-circle bearing: full credit within a tier-specific margin of the true bearing, tapering linearly to zero credit by a tier-specific threshold. This supersedes only the Compass-specific clause of ADR-0004 — Arrange's proportional credit and Border Run's binary scoring are unchanged. A direct consequence is that the generator no longer needs to reject pairs whose bearing lies too close to a discrete direction-boundary (IDEA.md's original "safe angular margin" rule): that rule existed solely to keep the 8-bucket answer unambiguous, and a continuous credit function has no such buckets to disambiguate. The trade-off is a materially more demanding interaction (players must judge an angle rather than recognize an obviously-correct button) and a scoring model with more tunable parameters (a full-credit margin and a zero-credit threshold per tier, instead of one fixed near-miss rule).
